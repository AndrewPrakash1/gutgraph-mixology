the fine print (it's short)

Educational. Not medical.

GutGraph is a flavour-discovery and meal-awareness project. It does not test your microbiome, diagnose anything, or replace a dietitian.

What it does

  • Reads your meal for fibre, ferments, sugar, spices and barrier protection with plain keyword rules you can see.
  • Suggests a can by the weakest score.
  • Shows shared aroma molecules between ingredients.

What it doesn't

  • No stool tests, no metagenomics, no clinical advice.
  • The culture curve and CFU numbers are illustrative.
  • Molecule counts come from the team's dataset, shown as given.

Reading list

  1. Wastyk et al., Cell 2021, fermented foods and microbiome diversity.
  2. Shoba et al., Planta Medica 1998, piperine and curcumin.
  3. Garg et al., Nucleic Acids Res. 2018, FlavorDB.
  4. Batra et al., Database 2020, RecipeDB.
